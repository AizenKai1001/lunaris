# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This is a small project and we want people to feel welcome to ask, report and contribute.

* Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people. No harassment, no insults, no discrimination.
* Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly. Beginners and non-native English speakers are welcome.
* Keep issues and pull requests on topic: a clear problem, what you expected, how to reproduce it.
* Do not post other people's private information.
* Contributions made with the help of AI tools are welcome when you have read, run and understood them, as for any other contribution.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues and block people who do not follow this. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use the repository's private "Report" options or contact a maintainer through the address on their GitHub profile.
